# International Strategy (Study Abroad)
Spring 2026 | Aalto University, Finland | Course code MNGT-E2009 | 6 ECTS | Level: ADVANCED (master's) | English | Faculty: Sarianna Lundan
Major Disciplines: Business / Strategy / International Business

> NOTE: An advanced, master's-level international strategy course taken abroad at Aalto University. Overshoots ES 212's introductory scope — evidence of business mastery well beyond "business basics."

## Learning Outcomes
Explores how firms can best start, develop, and manage their international activities. Covers the **internationalization process, entry strategy, entry mode choice, mergers and acquisitions, global supply chains, the management of international operations, and international negotiations.** Studies both small firms and large multinationals.

## Content — Questions Examined
1. Why and how do firms internationalize?
2. What challenges do firms face starting/developing/managing international operations, and how to overcome them?
3. Advantages/disadvantages of different **entry modes and internationalization strategies** and how to design/manage them
4. How to conduct **international negotiations** successfully
5. Strategies for managing international operations
6. How to design and manage **mergers and acquisitions**
7. Key issues for international supply chains

Strong practical focus, links theory and practice, includes industry guests. Self-study expected for students without prior business courses.

## Evaluation
Class participation; case study(s) (live or paper-based); group assessment; final individual report/project.

## Study Material
A set of readings and cases.
