# MO 302 — Positively Leading People and Organizations
Fall 2024 | Tue/Thu (two sections) | Instructor: Dr. Siyu Yu | Ross School of Business, University of Michigan (CORE course)

## Course Description
Enhances effectiveness as a leader and manager by introducing frameworks for understanding organizations and providing practical experience applying them. The field of management and organizations draws on economics, psychology, sociology, and political science to solve organizational problems and develop organizational competencies.

Management as "getting work done through people" — the course equips students with tools including **negotiation, networking, influence tactics, decision-making, and teamwork**, plus understanding why organizations are structured and function as they do (formal and informal structures, how they organize work, compensate people, their culture, and onboarding).

A key theme is **creating positive change in the business world**: the team project asks students to **propose an initiative within an established business — to act as an entrepreneur within a company** (intrapreneurship), fostering positive business change.
