# PSYCH 223 — Entrepreneurial Creativity
Instructor: Dr. Fretz | University of Michigan (Department of Psychology)

## Course Goals
1. Introduce basic psychological theories and constructs underlying **creativity** (including assessing personal skills/attributes related to creativity); establish a working definition of creativity and review research on characteristics of "Creatives."
2. Familiarize students with creativity and creative thinking in **collaborative settings**; psychometric self-assessments (personality, IQ, grit, etc.).
3. Develop **team skills and collaboration on creative tasks**; expose students to leadership in creative environments — leading and being led in creative collaborations.

## Three Phases
**Phase One — Creativity from the individual perspective:**
- Define creativity (distinct from intelligence, innovation, entrepreneurship, imagination), mini-C to Big-C
- Individual constructs: expertise, grit, "Big 5" personality, IQ, divergent thinking (with self-knowledge of own scores)
- Best practices for fostering creativity individually

**Phase Two — Creativity in teams and group work:**
- Key characteristics of high-performance teams
- Constructs for effective creative teams: Emotional Intelligence, Myers-Briggs, StrengthsFinder (with self-assessment)
- Work productively as a creative member of a team in a creative (possibly entrepreneurial) activity
- Best practices for fostering creativity on teams

**Phase Three — Leadership of creative teams:**
- Central tenets of good leadership; traits/skills for leading creative teams
- Reflect on and critique own and others' leadership/followership on a creative team

## Grading
- Reaction Papers (2 of 3) — 30 pts
- Participation (polling, discussion, scribe) — 30 pts
- **Group "Creative" Project — 40 pts:** team of 3–4 comes up with and implements a creative idea (can be a startup/business idea); weekly Friday section meetings, idea approval, checkpoints, final presentation, peer evaluation. Best presentations move to a special Ann Arbor entrepreneur-community event; finalists get an automatic A+.

## Relevance to ES 212 mapping
- **Innovation / creativity (ES 212 skills objective) — DIRECT, NAMED COURSE.** ES 212 lists "innovation / creativity" as an essential entrepreneurial skill and "formulate an innovation strategy and apply design skills." PSYCH 223 is an entire course on the psychology and practice of creativity and innovation, including a hands-on creative project.
- **Working on diverse teams / team skills (ES 212 skills objective; #11):** Phases Two and Three are entirely about team creativity, high-performance teams, and leadership of creative teams, with a graded team project and peer accountability.
- **Project management / collaboration (ES 212 skills):** semester-long team creative project with checkpoints and deliverables.
- FRAMING: strongest as evidence for the ES 212 *skills* objectives (innovation/creativity, teamwork, leadership) rather than a content unit. Complements the entrepreneurship courses by grounding the creativity/ideation foundation in psychological theory.
