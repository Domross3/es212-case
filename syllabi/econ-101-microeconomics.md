# ECON 101 — Principles of Economics I: Microeconomics
Summer 2024 | Remote, synchronous | MTuWTh 10:00–11:20am | Instructor: Adam Stevenson | University of Michigan (LSA Economics Department)

## Course Summary
Microeconomics is the study of individuals — people and firms — as they interact within an economy. It is the analysis of choice, subject to tradeoffs. Many interactions come in the form of **markets**, and the course studies the functions of markets — their successes and failures in the optimal use of social resources. Other interactions involve **government** and its regulatory tools. Students learn the fundamental tools and language of economists to understand human interaction, the nature of production, and the challenges of governing an economy.

Quantitative: students frequently perform calculations and represent mathematical relationships with graphs (Math 105 level recommended).

## Required Text
*Microeconomics*, Paul Krugman and Robin Wells (6th ed. or 4th/5th). Nearly the entire book is read.
