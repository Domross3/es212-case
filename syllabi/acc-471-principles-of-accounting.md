# ACC 471 — Principles of Accounting
Fall 2023 | Section 003 | Wed 6:00–9:00 PM | Instructor: Douglas (DJ) Stockbridge | Ross School of Business, University of Michigan

## Course Description
Covers both **financial and managerial accounting**, with an emphasis on an external/user perspective. **Designed for non-business-school students and business-minor students** who seek to understand how accounting information is used by business organizations, investors, and creditors. Answers questions such as:
- What types of information are included in financial statements?
- Manager: How can accounting information help me operate my organization more effectively?
- Business owner: How can I use accounting information to run my operations more profitably?
- Borrower: How will my banker use my accounting information when evaluating a loan request?
- Investor: How can I use financial information to make better investment decisions?

Students learn the basic rules companies follow when preparing financial statements; financial statements of publicly-traded companies are used frequently.

## Course Outline
**Part 1: The Accounting Cycle**
- What is accounting / types of accounting / financial statements / links among statements
- Cash vs. accrual basis; revenue and expense recognition
- Double-entry bookkeeping, journal entries, T-accounts

**Part 2: Assets**
- Current assets: cash, accounts receivable, inventory
- Long-term assets: PP&E, intangible assets, disposals

**Part 3: Financing Activities**
- Current liabilities
- Long-term financing (bonds)
- Stockholders' equity: capital stock, retained earnings, dividends, treasury stock

**Part 4: Using Accounting Information**
- **Income statement**
- **Statement of cash flows**
- **Financial statement analysis (ratios)**
- **Managerial accounting:** types of costs, budgeting, variance analysis
