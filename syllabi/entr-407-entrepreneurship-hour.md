# ENTR 407 — Entrepreneurship Hour
Winter 2024 | 1 Credit Hour, Pass/Fail | 11:30am–12:20pm | CFE Faculty: Eric Bacyinski | Center for Entrepreneurship, College of Engineering, University of Michigan

> EVIDENCE STRENGTH: SUPPLEMENTARY / LOWEST RANK. A 1-credit pass/fail weekly speaker seminar (~300 students). Reinforces entrepreneurship themes through exposure to practitioners; should rank BELOW all project-based and graded courses for every ES 212 unit. Use only as breadth/corroboration, never as a primary anchor.

## Course Overview
Weekly seminar series inviting disruptive, influential entrepreneurs, venture capitalists, and business leaders to speak about their experiences founding, financing, and managing startup ventures. Each session: a guest talk on their entrepreneurial-mindset journey, followed by a fireside chat and student Q&A.

In this elective you will:
- Understand the importance of entrepreneurship as a mindset for approaching problems and opportunities
- Develop an understanding of entrepreneurial characteristics presented by lecturers
- Interact with entrepreneurs and learn from their successes and failures

Speakers explore fundamental topics: emerging business models, ethics, new venture creation, business growth, and technology commercialization.

## Winter 2024 Course Topics
1. The journey of an entrepreneur
2. Managing a startup
3. Technology innovation
4. Financing, fundraising, venture capital
5. Launching a product
6. Business models

## Learning Outcomes
- Consider factors that enable or thwart the creation of companies / entrepreneurial careers
- Learn from lessons shared by leading entrepreneurs
- Present a framework on the complex decisions and tradeoffs entrepreneurs must make

## Grading
Pass/Fail. 100% attendance-based (weekly attention-check question). 70%+ = Pass. No projects or written deliverables.
