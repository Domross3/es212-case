# EECS 441 — Mobile App Development for Entrepreneurs
Winter 2026 | MW 3:00–4:20 + Fri discussion | Instructor: Elliot Soloway | GSI: Chenglin Li | College of Engineering (CSE), University of Michigan

## Course Description
Students engage in the hands-on practice of entrepreneurship by actually **inventing, building, testing, and marketing their own "mobile" apps**. A "mobile" app may mean: a Chrome extension, a web app, a native iOS/Android app, an Alexa Skill, an AI app, etc.

Class discusses: **best practices in the software engineering of mobile applications and best practices of software entrepreneurs in the design, production and marketing of mobile apps.**

Instructor's framing: "this is a course as much (if not more!) about entrepreneurship as it is about coding up apps." Programming is not taught — entrepreneurship practice is the core.

## Structure / Deliverables (project-based, team of 3)
- Pitch 1, Pitch 2 (venture pitching)
- Assignment #0: Project Analysis
- Assignment #1: Design Preso — 12%
- Assignment #2: **Customer Preso + MVP Design** — 12%
- Assignment #3: **MVP1 Live demo** — 12%
- Assignment #4: **MVP1 Result + MVP2 Design** — 12% (iterative product development based on results)
- Assignment #5: MVP2 Presentation — 12%
- **Company Preso** — 20%
- Class participation incl. peer reviews — 10%
- Team participation — variable

**Publication requirement: to get an A (A-, A, A+) the app MUST be published — on an app store, on a website, somewhere, with proof of publication.** (i.e., students must actually ship a real product to a real market.)

## Required Books
- **The Mythical Man-Month** — Fred Brooks (software project management, team scaling)
- **Crossing the Chasm** — Geoffrey Moore, 3rd Edition (technology adoption lifecycle, market segmentation, marketing strategy for technology products, mainstream market entry)

## Relevance to ES 212 mapping
- **Product design and development (#8):** the entire course spine — design presos, MVP1 → results → MVP2 iterative loop, live demos; customer feedback driving iteration.
- **Customer discovery / customer needs (#2, #8):** Customer Preso + MVP design grounded in customer analysis.
- **Market entry, marketing, selling, product/market fit (#9):** marketing of apps is in the course title; *Crossing the Chasm* = market entry strategy, segmentation, adoption; publication requirement forces an actual market launch.
- **Trends, technology, and the future (#3):** technology-product entrepreneurship; *Crossing the Chasm* technology adoption lifecycle; identifying opportunities in emerging tech (AI apps etc.).
- **Startup presentations and plans (#13/#5):** Pitch 1/2, five presentations, Company Preso.
- **Entrepreneurial teams (#11):** 3-person venture teams across a full build-and-launch cycle; *Mythical Man-Month* on team/project management; peer accountability for contribution.
- **Project management (ES 212 skills):** multi-milestone product delivery; *Mythical Man-Month*.
