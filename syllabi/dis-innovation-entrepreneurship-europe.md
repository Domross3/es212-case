# Innovation & Entrepreneurship in Europe (Study Abroad)
Summer 2024 | DIS Copenhagen (Denmark) | Core Course | Study Tour: Stockholm, Sweden | Faculty: Mikael Fuhr (Architect/Industrial Designer, Founder of FUHR)
Major Disciplines: Business, Entrepreneurship, Leadership Studies

> NOTE: This is Dom's entrepreneurship study-abroad course — an international startup incubator with a study tour to Sweden's entrepreneurial ecosystem.

## Course Description
Runs like a **startup incubator**: students form teams and create startups. Students learn to think like an innovator using frameworks like **Lean Startup, Business Model Canvas, Value Proposition Design, and Customer Journey Mapping**, with hands-on experience solving real-world problems and mentor guidance. Includes visits to startups in Sweden.

Process: identify a problem based on customer needs → develop hypotheses about customers and solutions → validate them through surveys, interviews, explainer videos, websites → **pitch to real investors and serial entrepreneurs** who treat students as future startup founders.

5.5 hours/week class + 11 hours/week team work.

## Learning Objectives
- Methods for identifying customer needs and continuously getting feedback on solutions
- **Developing a Business Model for a startup venture**
- **Building Minimum Viable Products and testing them with customers**
- **Preparing and delivering an effective investor pitch**
- Understanding that failure is inevitable in entrepreneurship but a source of learning
- Understanding the different actors in the entrepreneurial ecosystem (startups, scaleups, VCs, incubators/accelerators)
- Actively engaging in entrepreneurship communities in Denmark and Sweden

## Grading / Deliverables
- Engagement — 20% (indiv.)
- **Business Model Canvas + Customer Discovery/Validation + Market Analysis paper + presentation** — 15% (team)
- **MVP development and presentation** (live MVP: explainer video, landing page, app, prototype) — 15% (team)
- **Investor Pitches** (final + training pitches to professional investors) — 15% (team)
- 'Journey' and Reflection Paper — 35% (indiv.)

## Investor Pitch required content
Company name and purpose; Problem (Jobs/Gains/Pains); Solution (value proposition); How it works; Market analysis ("why now"); Revenue model; Team; **Financial projections**; the 'Ask'.
