## Course Overview
"Finding Your Venture provides a framework for identifying clear, impactful opportunities. Every student will leave with a viable business opportunity to pursue in addition to a set of valuable and repeatable skills that will be an asset in any entrepreneurial setting." Project-based, team-heavy. Instructor is a practicing entrepreneur who has started companies (not an academic).

By the end of the course you will:
- Know that you can start a business if you choose to
- Know what to do to get a new business started
- Have experience doing the things you'll need to do as an entrepreneur

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this course, students will be able to:
- **Identify five qualities of an attractive business-to-business customer**
- **Use customer interviews to find business-to-business opportunities**
- **Design and pitch a new product**
- **Prioritize and execute the most important steps to launch a business**

Students will:
- Interact with real potential customers outside the classroom
- Work with a small group in a startup-like environment
- Design a product or service that addresses a compelling opportunity
- **Learn how to sell your product to a customer by actually doing it**
- Gain an understanding of practical "how to start a business" fundamentals
- Learn to focus on the one thing that matters most
- Build a network of supporters and collaborators

## Course Schedule / Topics (proves specific coverage)
1. Setting expectations; "most important thing" (Start With Why)
2. **Business-to-business entrepreneurship & LineLeap simulation; business fundamentals**
3. Group formation & visioning; differentiation as advantage
4. **Talking to customers & ListeningFest; customer interview plan**
5. Field trip & practice customer interview
6. **Entrepreneurial Storytelling; intro to business models & pitching** (Pick Your Business Model With Intention) — Interview Write-Up
7. **Entrepreneurial Marketing; Internal Pitch Competition; "10 Business Models: 2 Pains x 5 solutions each"; Video Pitch w/ 5-Slide Pitch Deck** (Design a Product Your Customer Wants)
8. **Entrepreneurial Sales; Marketing Channel Brainstorm; 5-Slide Pitch Deck (team); Marketing Asset for Selling Your Product**
9. Selling in an authentic way
10. Final group presentations
11. "How do we know what to do next?" — Evaluate Opportunities Against a High Standard
12. **How to Launch Your Startups; "Get the Legal Basics Right"; Radical Prioritization**
13. Start-up-athon — Startup Launch Plan
14. Wrap-up; building community
